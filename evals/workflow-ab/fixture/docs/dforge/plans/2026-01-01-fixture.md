## Global Constraints

- Bash only; no files outside the repository root's scripts and `tests/`.
- Commits use `<type>(<scope>): <subject>`, subject at most 50 characters.

## Contracts

- C1 `greet.sh [--shout] [NAME]`: `--shout` prints the greeting in capitals.
- C2 `farewell.sh [NAME]`: prints `Goodbye, NAME!`, NAME defaulting to `world`.

## Assumptions (evidence)

- `greet.sh` takes the name as its first argument: `greet.sh` (read 2026-01-01).
